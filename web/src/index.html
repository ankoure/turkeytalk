Massachusetts · state setbacks, mapped

Can I hunt my turkey here?

Every acre in each city and town left over once the state's setbacks from homes and highways are erased, along with open water and public recreation land. Spatially possible isn't the same as permitted.

Weapon and setback
What this map does and doesn't know
  • Setbacks. Archery: no hunting within 500 ft of an occupied dwelling on someone else's land (MGL c.131 §58); 250 ft is the Governor's April 2026 proposal, still pending in the Legislature (S.3064), not law. Firearm: no discharge within 500 ft of a dwelling or any other building in use (MGL c.269 §12E). Both: nothing upon, across or within 150 ft of a state or hard-surfaced highway (§58). The owner or occupant can waive these; the map assumes nobody has.
  • Also erased, though not law. Open water, and public recreation and historic sites, on the assumption that their owner says no. No statute bars hunting on public land; DCR land is open by default unless listed in 302 CMR 12.11.
  • Thanksgiving. Fall turkey season 2026 (Zones 1–13) is archery-only on Thursday, Nov 26. Shotguns are legal only Oct 19–31.
  • Dwellings are buildings on parcels the assessor codes as residential, plus hotels, dorms, nursing homes, rectories and similar. Buildings on parcels with no use code are treated as dwellings.
  • Not on this map: town bylaws (except Cambridge's, applied on top of state law), seasons and zones, licenses and permits, legal days and hours, posted land, and safe lines of fire. Many cities and towns ban discharge outright.
  • Who to ask. Clicking candidate land looks up that spot's owner of record in MassGIS's statewide assessor parcels, live, and can draft a letter asking permission. This site stores no owner data, and what you type into a letter stays in your browser.
  • Every acre has an owner. MassWildlife lands are generally open to hunting under state rules. State law requires permission on private land only once it's posted (MGL c.131 §36), though some towns require written permission. Ask first anyway.

Data: MassGIS (structures, L3 parcels, protected open space, 1:25k hydrography, town boundaries), MassDOT Road Inventory. Owner lookups: MassGIS Property Tax Parcels, queried live. Analysis from a lightning talk at Fall NEARC 2026; method and code in the project repository.
